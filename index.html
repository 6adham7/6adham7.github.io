About

Hi, I'm 6art7.

I'm a Roblox SFX artist who builds custom sound effects, one layer at a time.

I don't rely on a single recording. Every effect is made from several parts: something low for weight, something sharper for detail, movement for life, and space to place it in the world. I edit, time and process each layer until they blend into one sound that feels right on screen.

I've been making SFX for Roblox games for over 1.5 years, and I like working closely with developers. I listen to what a moment should feel like, match the sound to the visuals and gameplay, and deliver clean, game-ready SFX that are easy to drop into a project.
